# DigiNode Tools sponsors

[DigiNode Tools](https://diginode.tools) lets anyone set up, monitor and manage a DigiByte Node, with no technical knowledge needed. It's free, its code is public so anyone can see exactly what it does, and I build and maintain it in my spare time. The more people who sponsor me, the more time I can devote to it. To see what your support helps with, and what I hope to work on next, visit [diginode.tools/sponsors](https://diginode.tools/sponsors/).

This repo keeps the list of sponsors up to date, for the website, the DigiNode Tools GitHub page, and DigiNode Tools itself.

## Featured Sponsors: adding your logo

On Ko-fi: join at the **Featured Sponsor** tier, then send me your logo, name and website in a Ko-fi message. On GitHub:

1. [Sponsor DigiNode Tools](https://github.com/sponsors/DigiNode-Tools) at the **Featured Sponsor** tier, with a **public** sponsorship.
2. Fill in the **[Become a Featured Sponsor](https://github.com/DigiNode-Tools/diginode-sponsors/issues/new?template=featured-sponsor.yml)** form. Give the name and website you want shown, and drag in your logo (PNG, JPG or WebP, under 1 MB, ideally at least 400 pixels wide on a transparent background).
3. Your request is checked automatically, usually within a minute. If anything needs fixing, a reply on your request explains what. Edit your request to check it again.
4. Once it's approved, your logo and link appear within a few minutes.

Your logo is shown on a white card, the same size as the other Featured Sponsors. If your company sponsors from an organisation account, enter it in the form. You need to be a [public member](https://docs.github.com/en/account-and-profile/setting-up-and-managing-your-personal-account-on-github/managing-your-membership-in-organizations/publicizing-or-hiding-organization-membership) of that organisation.

To change your logo, name or website later, just fill in the form again.

## Patrons: how you're listed

Patrons are listed automatically within a day of sponsoring, by name, linked to your GitHub profile. If you'd prefer your name without a link, or not to be listed at all, use the **[Change how I'm listed](https://github.com/DigiNode-Tools/diginode-sponsors/issues/new?template=patron-listing.yml)** form. It takes effect within a few minutes.

On Ko-fi, Patrons are listed automatically within a few minutes, by the name on your Ko-fi support, if you chose to make it public. To add a link to your website, or to not be listed, just send me a message on Ko-fi.
